About

The author, the series, the licence, and how to send a correction.

The author

Krimler writes about protocols and the systems built on top of them. This is the third book in a series about the Model Context Protocol, and the first one about taste rather than mechanism.

Corrections, disagreements, and reproduction failures are all welcome, and the last of those most of all. The entire premise of this series is that the claims are checkable, so a figure you cannot regenerate or a check you cannot reproduce is the most useful bug report available.

Colophon

Written in Markdown. The website is generated from that Markdown by tools/build_site.py, so there is exactly one copy of every sentence.

The annotated renders are captured from the companion gallery by headless Chrome, driven over the DevTools Protocol by tools/capture_figures.mjs. The hand-drawn figures are matplotlib, in xkcd mode, from scripts in figures-src/. The cover is generated by figures-src/cover.py, so its provenance is unambiguous and no third-party artwork is involved.

Prose rules are enforced by tools/lint_prose.py: no em dashes, a banned-phrase list, and near-duplicate sentence detection across the whole book.
